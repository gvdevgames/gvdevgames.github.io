Sudoku Classic Club

Sudoku Classic Club · Puzzle game for Android

Coming soon to Google Play
  • Works offline

  • 1 language
  • Free, Premium removes ads

Screenshots

Sudoku Classic Club is the classic number puzzle, done cleanly: a calm board, clear digits and the tools you expect, from your first Easy grid to Master puzzles that make you think.

FIVE DIFFICULTIES

  • Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert and Master, every puzzle with one solution
  • Best times, perfect games and win streaks for each level

DAILY CHALLENGE

  • A new puzzle every day, the same for everyone
  • A month calendar, a day streak and a trophy for each full month

KILLER SUDOKU

  • Cages with sums on top of the usual rules, for when the classic grid feels easy

HINTS THAT TEACH

  • A hint tells you why: the last cell in a row, the only digit that fits, the only place left
  • Eight short lessons walk you through the techniques, step by step

MORE TO PLAY

  • Weekly events with seven stages and a trophy
  • Puzzle packs with their own themes
  • 24 badges to earn

PLAY YOUR WAY

  • Notes with auto-remove, undo, eraser and number-first input
  • Mistake limit on or off
  • Light and dark mode, seven painted board themes
  • Calm music and soft sounds, both optional

Free with ads. Club Premium is an optional subscription that removes ads, gives unlimited hints and opens every pack and theme.
