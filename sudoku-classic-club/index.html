Sudoku Classic Club

Sudoku Classic Club · Puzzle game for Android

  • Works offline
  • No account
  • 12 languages
  • Free, Premium removes ads

Screenshots

Sudoku Classic Club is the classic number puzzle, done cleanly: a calm board, clear digits and the tools you expect, from your first Easy grid to Master puzzles that make you think.

FIVE DIFFICULTIES

  • Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert and Master, every puzzle with exactly one solution
  • Puzzles graded by the techniques they need, so every level feels like its name

DAILY CHALLENGE

  • A new puzzle every day, the same for everyone
  • A month calendar, a day streak and a trophy for every full month
  • Missed a day? A short video keeps your streak alive

KILLER AND VARIANTS

  • Killer Sudoku: cages with sums on top of the usual rules
  • Diagonal: both long diagonals hold every number once too
  • Windoku: four extra windows to fill
  • Mini 6×6 for a quick game

HINTS THAT TEACH

  • A hint comes in three steps: where to look, which cell, then the answer and why
  • Eight short lessons walk you through the techniques good solvers use

MORE TO PLAY

  • Weekly events with seven puzzles and a trophy
  • Puzzle packs with their own themes
  • 24 badges, also as Google Play Games achievements, and leaderboards

PLAY YOUR WAY

  • Notes with auto-remove, undo and redo, an eraser and number-first input
  • Colour cells to mark them your way
  • Mistake limit on or off; on Expert and Master you can play without checking until the board is full
  • Statistics for every mode and level: win rate, best and average times, streaks and your recent times
  • Large numbers, colour-blind help and a left-handed layout
  • Light and dark mode, seven painted board themes, on phones and tablets
  • Calm music and soft sounds, both optional
  • An evening reminder for the daily challenge, if you want one

Free with ads. Club Premium is an optional monthly subscription: no ads, unlimited hints, second chances without a video, and every pack and board theme.
