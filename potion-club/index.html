Potion Club

Potion Club: Match 3 Shop · Match-3 puzzle for Android

Coming soon to Google Play
  • Works offline
  • No account
  • 5 languages
  • Free, Premium removes ads

Screenshots

Potion Club is a cosy match-3 game with a shop at its heart. You have inherited your grandmother's little apothecary on Lantern Lane. Every board is an order for a customer: swap the ingredients, line up three or more, gather what the potion needs before your moves run out, and watch the cauldron brew it.

MATCH AND BREW

  • Seven ingredients, from Moonpetal to Sea pearl, on boards of many shapes
  • Flasks, bombs and essences: power tiles that clear rows, burst and gather, and combine into something bigger
  • A cauldron that heats up as you play: tap it when it is full and it boils over the board
  • Cobwebs, frost, thick ice, crates, creeping mould, spider nests, locked chests and bottles to bring down to the counter
  • Rush orders against the clock and recipes brewed one ingredient at a time
  • Three tools on the tray for a tight spot, and stars for every move you have to spare

A SHOP THAT GROWS

  • A bigger cauldron, shelves, a herb garden, a brass lamp and a shop cat
  • Decorate the walls, the counter, the lamp light and the wall above the counter; some looks cannot be bought, only earned
  • A recipe book of 42 potions, each with a line of its own, and gifts for filling it
  • A shelf by the door that sells your spare bottles while you are away
  • Slow brews that simmer for hours, and a cat that goes out over the rooftops and seldom comes home empty-pawed

A TOWN OF NEIGHBOURS

  • Sixteen regulars with their own voices: win their hearts and they tell you their stories and write you letters
  • Six streets that open one by one, each with new neighbours and something new on the boards
  • A visitor every week with three orders and a keepsake for the shop
  • Titles for the shop, from Apprentice to Legend of the lane

SOMETHING EVERY DAY

  • A daily order, the same for everyone, with a calendar and a streak
  • A weekly festival of seven orders and a cup
  • Three small tasks a day, a stamp card and a chest
  • Seasons in the sky over the town, each with a wreath to earn
  • Reminders when a brew is ready or the cat is home, only if you want them

PLAY YOUR WAY

  • No lives and no waiting: play as many orders as you like
  • An order left half-way waits on the counter
  • Works offline, on phones and tablets
  • Calm music and soft sounds, both optional; a calm board without shakes or sparks

Free with ads. Potion Club Premium is an optional monthly subscription: no ads, one more move on every order, double tips, a free second chance and the Aurora wall for your shop.
