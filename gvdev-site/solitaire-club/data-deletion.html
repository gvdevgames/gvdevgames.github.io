Data deletion

Solitaire Club keeps your games, statistics, settings and Premium status only on your device. GV Dev
runs no servers and holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our
side. Here is how to remove everything that exists:

Data on your device

  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data: games in progress, statistics, settings and the

    • locally cached Premium status.

  • If Android backed the app up to your Google account, delete that backup under Google account

    • settings > Backups, or simply uninstall and reinstall the app.

Advertising ID (used by Google AdMob in the free version)

  • Android settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads) lets you reset the advertising ID or delete

    • it entirely. Google's own retention of ad data is described at policies.google.com/privacy.

Play Games profile and leaderboard scores (only if you signed in)

  • Your gamertag, avatar and posted scores belong to your Google Play Games profile. Open the

    • Google Play Games app > Settings > Delete Play Games account and data, or contact Google
    through support.google.com/googleplay.

Purchases

  • Purchase records for the Premium subscription are held by Google Play under your Google

    • account. Manage or cancel the subscription at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

Anything else

Email gvdevgames@gmail.com from the address you want us to answer, and we will help within 30 days.
