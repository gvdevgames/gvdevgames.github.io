Solitaire Club

Solitaire Club: Card Games · Card game for Android

  • Works offline
  • No account
  • 21 languages
  • Free, Premium removes ads

Screenshots

Solitaire Club is a small, quiet solitaire app that looks the part: Klondike, Spider and FreeCell on hand-painted tables, with nothing standing between you and the cards.

LIGHT AND FAST

  • A download of about 12 MB, tiny on your phone
  • Opens instantly, no loading screens, no sign-in
  • Works completely offline: on a plane, in the metro, anywhere

QUIET BY DESIGN

  • No notifications, no pop-ups, no "come back and play" nagging
  • No account and no tracking of our own
  • The free version shows at most one short ad every 10 minutes, always announced with a countdown first, never in the middle of a move, and never a banner over your cards

THREE CLASSICS, DONE PROPERLY

  • Klondike – draw 1 or draw 3
  • Spider – 1, 2 or 4 suits, from relaxing to expert
  • FreeCell – every card face up, nearly every deal winnable

Undo, hints, auto-complete, and automatic safe moves to the foundations.

TABLES THAT LOOK THE PART

  • 15 painted tables: Classic Green, Midnight Blue, Burgundy, Royal Purple, Graphite, Ocean Teal, Wood Study, Deep Space, Autumn and Art Deco, plus five Premium tables
  • Every table is painted for both portrait and landscape, so it never looks stretched
  • A soft shade under the controls keeps the score and buttons readable on any table

CARDS MADE FOR A PHONE SCREEN

  • Large, crisp faces with pips you can read at a glance, in portrait and landscape
  • Painted, double-ended court cards: the king, queen and jack look right whichever way the card lies
  • 11 card backs: crimson and navy ornaments, emerald damask, black-and-gold deco, teal floral, purple mandala, blue and red lattice, emerald rings, midnight spade and gold deco
  • Colour-blind friendly: a four-colour deck gives clubs and diamonds their own colours, so the suits never get mixed up
  • A left-handed layout, and smooth animations with a switch to turn them off

STATISTICS THAT MEAN SOMETHING

Every game and every mode keeps its own record: games played and won, win rate, best time, fewest moves, current and longest streak. Klondike draw-1 and draw-3 are counted separately, as are Spider's 1, 2 and 4 suits, so a hard mode never dilutes an easy one.

A DAILY DEAL AND ONLINE LEADERBOARDS

One shared Klondike deal every day, the same cards for everyone. Beat it, keep your daily streak alive, and see where your time lands on the daily leaderboard. Klondike, Spider and FreeCell each have their own best-time leaderboard too. Sign in with Google Play Games when you want to compete; the game is complete without it.

SOLITAIRE PREMIUM

An optional monthly subscription removes ads and unlocks five exclusive tables: Winter Frost, Neon Nights, Sakura, Jungle and Leather Desk. Cancel any time in Google Play.

Just cards, felt and a quiet moment.
