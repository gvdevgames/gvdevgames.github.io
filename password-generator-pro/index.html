Password Generator Pro

Password Generator Pro & Vault · Utility for Android

  • Works offline
  • No account
  • 21 languages
  • Free, no ads

Screenshots

Password Generator Pro makes strong random passwords and keeps them behind a PIN, entirely on your phone. No account, no ads and no internet permission: nothing you generate or save can leave the device.

STRONG PASSWORDS IN ONE TAP

  • 5 to 50 characters, with capital letters, small letters, digits and symbols in any mix
  • Edit the symbol set for sites that reject certain characters
  • Skip look-alike characters (0 O o 1 l I |) for passwords you have to read out or type by hand
  • Up to 20 passwords at once
  • A strength meter in entropy bits, so you know what you are getting
  • Copy with one tap; the clipboard clears itself after a time you choose

A VAULT BEHIND YOUR PIN

  • Save a password with the service name, login and a note; search and copy it later
  • A 4 to 8 digit PIN on the app's own keypad
  • Saved passwords are encrypted with AES-256; the key is protected by your PIN and by the phone's secure hardware, so the data cannot be read on another device or without the PIN
  • Growing waits after wrong PINs
  • Auto-lock when you leave the app, screenshots blocked, a blank card in recent apps

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

  • No internet permission at all: the app cannot connect, so there is nothing to take on trust
  • No ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no account
  • Not included in Android cloud backups
  • Free, with everything included

YOUR DATA, YOUR FILE

Export an encrypted backup protected by a password you choose, and import it on a new phone or after a reset. That file is the only way your passwords ever leave the app, and only when you say so.

A note on forgotten PINs: the PIN is part of the encryption key and cannot be recovered or reset. If you forget it, the only way forward is to erase the app's data and restore from a backup file. Keep one.

Dark and light themes. Available in 21 languages.
