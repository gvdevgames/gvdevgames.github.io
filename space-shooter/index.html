Space Shooter

Space Shooter Galaxy Adventure · Arcade shooter for Android

  • Works offline
  • No account
  • 10 languages
  • Free, Premium removes ads

Screenshots

Space Shooter Galaxy Adventure is an arcade space shooter for one thumb: drag anywhere to fly, the ship fires by itself, and the waves keep coming. Dodge, blast, collect what the enemies drop, and fly a little further every run.

HOW IT PLAYS

  • Drag to fly; the guns fire by themselves
  • Ten kinds of enemy in formations, elites worth triple, asteroid fields, swarms and convoys
  • Three bosses, the Dreadnought, the Hive and the Vortex, each with attacks of its own
  • Power-ups: more firepower, shields, repairs, bombs, homing rockets, wingmen and gold rushes
  • Weapon modes for a while: a spread of three, a beam that passes through, homing shots
  • Fill the overdrive by destroying enemies, then let it loose: untouchable, and firing twice as fast
  • Kills in a row raise the score multiplier; a near miss pays a bonus
  • One continue per run

YOUR SHIP, YOUR PILOT

  • Four ships with guns and hulls of their own: Vanguard, Striker, Bulwark and Phantom
  • Fourteen coats and six skies to fly under
  • A web of 42 skills in six branches: firepower, overdrive, score, support, fortune and armour. Every pilot level is a skill point
  • Ranks from Cadet to Admiral

THE GALAXY

  • A route of eight planets, each with a guardian to beat before the next one opens
  • Start later runs from any planet you have reached
  • Five resources drop from enemies and rocks: ferrite, crystal, plasma, alloy and dark matter
  • A workshop of nine parts in three marks, fitted in three slots and paid in resources only: spread, beam and homing guns, plating, a shield generator, reactive plating, a tractor coil, a drone bay and a mining rig

EVERY DAY

  • A daily challenge with two twists, the same waves for everyone
  • Boss rush: the bosses one after another
  • Custom runs: turn on twists for more points
  • Missions, a seven-day login gift and a season pass with a track of coins, coats and skies

RECORDS

  • Statistics, twelve medals, and Google Play Games leaderboards and achievements if you sign in

PREMIUM AND ADS

The game shows a small banner on the menu, which you can close, and offers a video for a continue and for a daily bonus of coins; nothing interrupts a run. Premium is a monthly subscription that removes the banner and the continue video, opens the premium track of the season pass, paints every ship in the Royal purple-and-gold livery with a sky to match, and adds coins to every daily gift.

Works offline. Sound, music and vibration can be switched off.
