Alias: Word Club

Alias: Word Club – Party Game · Party game for Android

  • Works offline
  • No account
  • 5 languages
  • Free, one-time unlock

Screenshots

Alias: Word Club is a party word game for one phone and a room full of friends. One player explains the word on the card without saying it, the teammates guess, and the clock is ticking. Swipe up for guessed, down to skip, and pass the phone to the next team.

HOW A GAME GOES

  • Split into 2 to 6 teams, or play as two with one shared score and a record to beat
  • Pick the word language, a difficulty and the themes you like
  • Each team gets a turn of 30 to 120 seconds; the first to reach the target wins
  • After every turn, check the list and fix any mark with a tap

WORDS THAT KEEP COMING

  • Over 5,000 words in each language: English, Spanish, German, French and Ukrainian
  • Easy, medium, hard or mixed
  • Themes: General, Food, Animals, Travel, Sports, Science and Culture are included; Movies & TV, Music, Jobs, Geography, Holidays, Sayings and a cheeky Party 18+ pack come with the one-time purchase, together with your own word list
  • Words you have played do not come back until the whole deck has been used
  • Family mode: simple everyday words and gentler rules for mixed ages

YOUR RULES

  • Turn time, points to win, and whether a skipped word costs a point
  • Last word for everyone: when time runs out, any team may guess the word on the card
  • Steal skipped words: another team can take a skipped word for a point

MADE FOR THE TABLE

  • Big readable cards, a timer you can see from across the room, and a pulse through the last ten seconds
  • Pass-the-phone screens, so nobody peeks
  • A scoreboard with team characters, statistics, and the words your group skips the most
  • Four colour themes; sound and vibration you can switch off
  • Works offline, no account needed

ADS AND THE PURCHASE

The game shows a small banner on the start screen and one full-screen ad after a game, never during a turn. A single one-time purchase removes the ads and opens all the themes and your own words for good. You can also watch a short video to open the extra themes for the evening.

Alias: Word Club is an independent game and is not affiliated with any board game publisher.
