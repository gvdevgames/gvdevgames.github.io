Memory Training

Memory Training: Brain Games · Memory games for Android

Coming soon to Google Play
  • Works offline
  • No account
  • 14 languages
  • Free, Premium removes ads

Screenshots

Memory Training is a collection of fourteen short memory games with one aim: a few minutes a day that help you remember more. Every game is a run: a board shows for a few seconds, hides, and you answer. Clear a level and the next one adds a little more. Three lives a run, and your best level in each game earns you ranks, from Rookie to Grandmaster.

THE GAMES

  • Number Order: memorize where the numbers are, then tap them in order
  • Pattern Grid: cells light up, then go dark; tap them back
  • Sequence: pads play a tune that grows by one step every level
  • What's Missing: a tray of pieces, one vanishes, the rest shuffle
  • Pairs: cards show face up, then flip; find the twins
  • Faces & Names: meet a few people, then put the right name to each face
  • Shopping List: groceries go by one at a time; pick them off the full shelf
  • Where Was It: pieces on a grid, one moves; tap where it used to be
  • Phone Number: digits go by; type the number back
  • Who Wore What: pieces turn grey; give each one its colour back
  • Trail: a dot walks a path; walk it again, point by point
  • Lineup: pick the faces you met out of a lineup with strangers
  • Add Up: numbers flash by; keep the running total
  • Two Back: pieces stream by; tap when one matches the piece two steps before

Every game reaches level 18, and most of them hide a twist (Reverse, Rotate, Shuffle, Swap, Triplets and more) that opens once you reach Sharp eye in the classic game.

EVERY DAY

  • Today's run: one game a day with the same boards for everyone, and your standing against other players
  • Weekly challenge: the same boards for everyone all week
  • Streaks and weekly goals, with a colour set to unlock
  • Workouts: four games drawn for the day, about three minutes, one score; a long workout and three themed packs with Premium

YOUR MEMORY SCORE

A baseline test of three games sets your starting score, and a monthly check-up on the same three games shows how far you have come. The stats pages keep your memory age, a thirty-day chart, your best and average level in every game, your recent runs and your daily results.

MORE WAYS TO PLAY

  • Blitz: one life, a quicker pace, its own record
  • Zen: no lives to lose, start at any level (with Premium)
  • Share any run as a link: your friend plays the very same boards
  • 22 achievements, also on Google Play Games
  • 24 memory techniques, one after every run: chunking, the memory palace, stories, rhythm and more

EASY ON THE EYES

Painted pieces, faces and groceries on a quiet night sky, five colour sets (Classic, Contrast, Pastel, Neon and Aurora), two piece styles, three music loops and soft sound effects. Every board is labelled for screen readers.

PREMIUM

Memory Training is supported by a small banner on the menu and a full-screen ad between runs at most every ten minutes, never inside a run. Premium, a monthly subscription, removes the ads and brings every colour set, the jelly pieces, unlimited workouts, Zen mode and the extras (a second chance, a saved streak) without videos. You can try it for an hour by watching a short video.
