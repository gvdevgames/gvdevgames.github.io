Mahjong Club

Mahjong Club: Tile Match Game · Tile game for Android

  • Works offline
  • No account
  • 21 languages
  • Free, Premium removes ads

Screenshots

Mahjong Club is a classic mahjong solitaire game: match pairs of free tiles to clear the board, on hand-painted tables, with nothing between you and the tiles. A quiet, relaxing tile-matching puzzle that is light, works offline and never nags.

LIGHT AND FAST

  • A download of about 10 MB
  • Opens instantly, no loading screens, no sign-in
  • Works completely offline: on a plane, in the metro, anywhere

QUIET BY DESIGN

  • No pop-ups and no "come back and play" nagging; the daily reminder stays off unless you turn it on
  • No account and no tracking of our own
  • The free version shows one small banner under the menu, which you can close, and a short full-screen ad after every third win, at most one every ten minutes, always announced with a countdown and never over the board

87 LAYOUTS, 36 SHAPES

  • Boards from 22 to 92 tiles: pyramid, turtle, dragon, fortress, pagoda, phoenix, koi, mountain and many more
  • Fewer, bigger tiles per board, so every face is readable on a phone
  • Every deal is solvable; undo, hint and shuffle are there when you get stuck
  • New groups of layouts open as you win; Premium opens them all at once

A DAILY DEAL AND A WEEKLY CHALLENGE

The same tiles for everyone, every day, with a streak calendar to keep alive. Each week a big board with its own leaderboard. Sign in with Google Play Games to compete on three leaderboards and collect 13 achievements; the game is complete without it.

TABLES AND TILES THAT LOOK THE PART

  • 13 painted tables: lacquer, jade silk, indigo cloud, ink wash, bamboo grove and more, plus three seasonal tables that are free while their season lasts
  • Three tile styles (ivory, jade, midnight), a bold high-contrast face set for small screens and colour-blind players, and three tile sizes
  • Painted flower and season tiles

STATISTICS THAT MEAN SOMETHING

Games played and won, win rate, best time, fewest shuffles, fewest undos, best score and streaks, per layout and overall.

MAHJONG PREMIUM

An optional monthly subscription removes ads, opens every level and unlocks five exclusive tables: gold lattice, plum blossom, black stone, sunset silk and sakura night. Cancel any time in Google Play.

Just tiles, lacquer and a quiet moment.
